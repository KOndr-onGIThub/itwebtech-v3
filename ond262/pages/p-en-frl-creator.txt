FRL Creator
A PDF generator for shelf information sheets
Book a consultation
FRL Creator is an application that generates shelf information sheets in PDF for Toyota Kolín, based on variable data. The user enters a period, imports data, validates the result, and selects a sheet group — the output is a ready PDF for printing.
Challenge
Creating shelf labels was done manually — slow, error-prone, and dependent on one particular person. The team needed a system that fits into the process as a reliable partner: accepts parameters, imports current data, and generates a consistent PDF for print.
Solution
A web application with a parameter-entry interface, external data import, validation, and PDF output. The work began with process analysis, requirement mapping, and a clear milestone plan. Findings during testing were resolved quickly.
Result
The client gained a tool that saves time and reduces errors when producing shelf labels. The review highlights "a professional approach to work", thorough process analysis, and rapid resolution of findings during testing (5/5).

Elektro Srnák — Lukáš Srnák
Elektro Srnák
A small website for an electrician that wastes no time
Book a consultation
Lukáš Srnák needed a small calling card stating what he does and what he rents out. The micro-site is minimalist on the surface, with proper technical hygiene under the hood — Schema markup, OpenGraph, Google Analytics, Search Console, Maps integration. Note: the site has been offline since mid-2025 (client ended hosting).
Challenge
The client didn't need a big website, but the small one had to deliver. Local customers search for electricians on mobile — the site had to be fast, scannable, and easy to find on Google. Without SEO basics, it would have been lost.
Solution
A minimalist site with the essential sections (services, equipment rental, contact). Underneath: technical hygiene — local-business Schema, OG metadata, Google Search Console and Analytics integration, photo lightbox, and a custom logo. Nothing extra — and nothing search engines expect is missing.
Result
The client got a functional calling card at a reasonable cost — it leads users to contact quickly and behaves correctly in Google. Review: 5/5 stars highlighting speed, willingness, and fair pricing. The site was online until mid-2025, when the client ended hosting.

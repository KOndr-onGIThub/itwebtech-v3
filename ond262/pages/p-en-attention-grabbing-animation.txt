Attention-grabbing animation
A sample of how to break through a saturated feed
Book a consultation
An in-house animation I use as a reference when discussing social media promotion with clients. Video is almost a necessity in a saturated feed today — but only when it's well-targeted and has something to say in the first seconds.
Challenge
Most social content scrolls past unnoticed. A static post isn't enough — the brain files it as noise. Something is needed that breaks through that noise.
Solution
An animation built to have motion already in the thumbnail, a simple message, and pacing tuned for the mobile feed. It works as a sample I can show clients to make the concept of video promotion concrete.
Result
I have a reference format that shows clients exactly what their promotion could look like. Instead of an abstract debate about "let's do video", there's a tangible piece — and so a tangible decision.

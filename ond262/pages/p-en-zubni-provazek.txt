Zubní Provázek
Dental practice redesign with local SEO focus
Visit live site
Book a consultation
MDDr. Václav Provázek runs a dental practice in Hrabětice. The old site looked dated, didn't work on mobile, and left patients without basic answers. I rebuilt it with a focus on readable information, transparent pricing, FAQ, and local search visibility.
Challenge
The site looked untrustworthy, mobile use was painful, and treatment and pricing information was missing. It was also essentially invisible in Google. For a medical practice the first impression is decisive — most patients find their dentist online.
Solution
A modern responsive site with a full overview of treatments, indicative pricing, and FAQ. I added an educational blog with three intro articles on dental care that the client can extend. Local SEO targets keywords like "dentist Hrabětice" and "dental hygiene Hrabětice".
Result
The practice ranks first in Google for its key local search, "zubař Hrabětice", and Google brings most of all traffic — 460 of 524 visits (Google Analytics, 1 Jan – 16 Sep 2026). The site answers what patients used to have to ask over the phone: prices, treatment overview, opening hours and location.

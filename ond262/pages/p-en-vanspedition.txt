VAN spedition
A logistics micro-site with a single clear path to contact
Visit live site
Book a consultation
VAN spedition needed a simple, professional presentation. A micro-site built around the essentials — company intro, services, advantages, contact — with no filler that would stand between the visitor and the only action that matters: getting in touch.
Challenge
A freight forwarder doesn't need a sprawling site with twenty subpages. Clients need to know fast what you do, who runs it, and how to reach you. The goal was to put everything that matters on one page without overloading the visitor.
Solution
A micro-site with a clear structure and a focus on speed. Main information, key advantages, and contact — all in view. The visual feels correct and professional, which matters for B2B logistics clients.
Result
The client has a working presentation at a reasonable cost. The site serves as a credible link in communication and simplifies first contact. A 5/5 review confirms that the "do the most for the client" approach worked here.

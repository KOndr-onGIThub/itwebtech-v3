Josef Opa
Josef Opa
A multilingual website for a construction firm with its own brand
Visit live site
Book a consultation
Josef Opa handles renovations, modernisations, and full builds. He works mainly in Germany, so he needed a presentation that works on both German- and English-speaking markets. I built a bilingual website and designed the logo, e-mail identity, and business cards with a QR code.
Challenge
The client needed a credible nameplate as a solid local construction partner in Germany. Without his own brand, website, or consistent identity, he didn't stand out anywhere. Multilingual support was essential — most communication happens in German, but enquiries also come in English.
Solution
A responsive multilingual website, a bold playful logo, a professional e-mail on a custom domain, and business cards with a QR code that points straight to the site. Subtle animations enhance the experience without slowing the site down.
Result
The firm now has one consistent identity across web, e-mail, and printed cards. The client looks credible on both language markets, the @josefopa e-mail address adds weight to written communication, and the QR-code card makes handing over contact info a single move.

Carpentry, roofing and masonry in one focused website

Střechy Zajíc do carpentry, roofing and masonry — a trade where customers choose by references and photos. The site had to deliver: a gallery of completed jobs, clear service descriptions, and a logo that strengthens the brand.
Challenge
The client needed a presentation that proves quality of work more convincingly than a few Facebook photos. The job was to structure the services (roof reconstruction, carpentry, building work), set up a reference gallery, and add trade articles that support SEO and trust.
Solution
A responsive site with a service catalogue, detailed descriptions, and a photo gallery of completed projects. Plus a logo, content optimisation, and trade articles. The site works equally well on desktop and the phone where builders typically check "that guy, what's his name".
Result
The client has a solid presentation that represents the craft instead of looking like a budget construction page. Jobs come in based on the reference gallery, the site supports local search, and the brand stands stronger against template-based competitors.

YOLK Studio (Peter Vidlička)
YOLK — agency development on clinic portals
Agency-side development and maintenance on healthcare portals
Book a consultation
For YOLK studio I provide ongoing development and fixes on large client websites — the patient portal unica.cz and the reproductive clinic site reproclinic.com. The work includes seasonal updates, form changes, graphics tuning, and custom features that go beyond standard WordPress.
Challenge
The agency manages clients with large portals where any small broken piece directly hurts patient experience. The site needed reliable upkeep, seasonal updates to offers and questionnaires, and custom features that WordPress alone can't deliver.
Solution
I work as an external developer on specific tasks — seasonal changes, bug fixes, custom PHP/JS extensions, and form edits. Communication runs directly with the agency team; for the agency's clients the output is invisible in the good sense — everything just works.
Result
The agency gained a reliable technical partner for jobs that need experienced development. Review: "Ondra is a very reliable and skilled developer; cooperation has always gone well." (5/5)

PitArena (Stanislav Holcmann)
Article on Motorkáři.cz
Content marketing that reached the country's biggest moto portal
Visit live site
Book a consultation
For PitArena I wrote, free of charge, an article on how YCF Cup is pushing motocross pit bikes forward. It went out on the client's blog and, after a small editorial pass, on Motorkáři.cz — the biggest Czech moto portal, where the client's audience actually hangs out.
Challenge
PitArena needed content that travels beyond its own website. The goal was an article that would make sense for local fans and for a major moto portal — and that would actually reach their readers.
Solution
The piece reads from inside the scene and explains how YCF Cup is changing pit bikes and why the series is winning over riders and fans. The original went on pitarena.cz; an edited version made it onto Motorkáři.cz under the hand of an experienced editor.
Result
PitArena got attention outside its own bubble — the brand appeared on the country's biggest moto portal and the client gained solid content for their own blog. That's exactly what content marketing does when someone gets it right.
